How to install

  1. Tap Download APK above. Use Chrome or your phone's browser.
  2. If a warning says the file may be harmful, tap Download anyway or OK.
  3. When the download finishes, tap Open. Or open the Files or Downloads app and tap mmk-gold-price.apk.
  4. If the phone says it cannot install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press Back.
  5. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap Install anyway.
  6. Tap Open. Prices load straight away, with no sign-up.

On Android 7 or older, go to Settings → Security and turn on Unknown sources. After installing, you can turn the permission off again. An APK does not update by itself: to get a newer version, download the APK here again.
