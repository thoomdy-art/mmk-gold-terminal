About Us

Welcome to မြန်မာ့ ရွှေဈေး (Myanmar Gold Price). Our platform is dedicated to providing real-time, highly accurate financial data for the Myanmar market. We understand the complexity of local gold trading, which involves converting global Troy Ounces into local Kyat, Pae, and Yway measurements across both Old and New market standards.

By aggregating live spot prices from trusted global exchanges like OANDA and cross-referencing them with local Peer-to-Peer (P2P) currency rates, we empower merchants, buyers, and financial analysts with transparent tools to make informed decisions.
