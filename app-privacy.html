Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Myanmar Gold Price Android app (package space.wemanage.mmkgold) and the website wemanage.space (“we”, “our”), and how it is used. By using the app or the site you agree to this policy.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, sign-up forms or comment sections. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location or financial details. The numbers you type into the converters and calculators are processed on your device and are not sent to our server.

Information collected automatically

To show gold prices the app requests data from our server (hosted on Render) and from public price sources. Like any internet service, these servers can receive technical data such as your IP address, device or browser type and the time of the request. We use it to deliver the service, keep it secure and measure usage. We do not build user profiles and do not sell this data.

Data stored on your device

The app and the site remember your language and light/dark choice on your device. This contains no personal data and is not sent to us.

Advertising (Start.io)

The app shows ads provided by Start.io (formerly StartApp). To select and show ads, the Start.io SDK inside the app may automatically collect and share with Start.io and its advertising partners: your Android advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, operating system version, language, carrier and network type), and information about how you interact with ads. Where your advertising ID is available, Start.io may also collect the list of apps installed on your device. This data is used for showing and measuring ads, including personalised ads.

  • Start.io’s own privacy policy: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/
  • Choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings (Privacy > Ads), and you can opt out of personalised ads there. You can also ask Start.io to stop processing your data by contacting support@start.io. Uninstalling the app removes the SDK from your device.
  • Consent. Where required by law, Start.io may ask for your consent when you first open the app.

Third-party services

To work, the app and the site request data from or load content provided by third parties, each with its own privacy policy: Start.io (ads), TradingView (embedded chart and OANDA price data), Binance, OKX and Bybit (public price data), Google Fonts (website only), jsDelivr and Tailwind CDN (website scripts), and our own backend hosted on Render. We do not control their practices.

Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers described above, who receive technical data as part of delivering the app and its ads.

Children

The app and the site are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the service, but no internet service is perfectly secure. We do not keep a user database. Technical logs on our hosting provider are kept only as long as needed for security and operation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to admin@wemanage.space or visit our contact page.
