Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, sign-up forms or comment sections. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location or financial details. The numbers you type into the converters and calculators are processed on your device and are not sent to our server.

Information collected automatically

To show gold prices, the app requests data from our server (hosted on Render) and from public price sources. Like any internet service, these servers can receive technical data such as your IP address, device or browser type and the time of the request. We use it to deliver the service, keep it secure and measure usage. We do not build user profiles.

Data stored on your device

The app and the site remember your language and light/dark choice on your device. This contains no personal data and is not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To select, show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK in the app may automatically collect and share with Google and its advertising partners:

  • your Android advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version, network type),
  • information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs.

This data is used to show and measure ads, including personalised ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google’s use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone’s Settings (Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).
  • Manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the SDK from your device.

Third-party services

To work, the app and the site request data from or load content provided by third parties, each with its own privacy policy: Google AdMob (ads), TradingView (embedded chart and OANDA price data, which may set its own cookies or identifiers), Binance, OKX and Bybit (public price data), Google Fonts (website only), jsDelivr and Tailwind CDN (website scripts), and our own backend hosted on Render. We do not control their practices.

Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal information ourselves. Our advertising provider, Google AdMob, and its partners receive the advertising data described above in order to deliver ads; under some laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, this may be considered “sharing” for advertising. Other than the service providers described in this policy, we do not share your information with anyone.

International transfers

Our service providers, including Google and Render, may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, they rely on legal safeguards for these transfers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Thailand’s PDPA or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. We do not hold a user database, so most data about you is held by Google and the other providers above; you can use the controls in “Your choices” or contact them directly. For anything we hold, write to admin@wemanage.space and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The app and the site are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children and do not knowingly show them personalised ads.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the service, but no internet service is perfectly secure. We do not keep a user database. Technical logs on our hosting provider are kept only as long as needed for security and operation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to admin@wemanage.space or visit our contact page.
